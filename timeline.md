A chronological overview of canonical events in the ESF universe.

---

## 1947

**July 7, 1947** - [The Beginning Is A Delicate Time](/esf/stories/the-beginning)
- Roswell Incident occurs
- Sergeant Nathaniel Summers discovers crashed alien craft
- General John Haytham begins initial investigation
- Foundation event leading to EarthSpace Federation formation

---

## 2019

**May 5, 2019** - [The Cormorant Gambit](/esf/stories/the-cormorant-gambit)
- Experimental fast destroyer FSS *Cormorant* (Commander Helen Voss) plays dead in an asteroid field to bait three Ryzak frigates
- Two frigates destroyed in seconds; the third, under Commander Vrak Tohlek, run down at non-survivable acceleration
- Xenari Envoy Zhaeleth observes aboard to evaluate the new destroyer design
- Zhaeleth's report to the Great Circle warns that humans fight "without limits"

---

## 2021

**March 21, 2021** - [The Wraith](/esf/stories/the-american-wraith)
- CIA asset "Nightingale" (Marcus Adams) compromised in Moscow
- Alicia Monroe makes desperate plea to Federation
- First documented Federation covert extraction operation on behalf of the CIA
- Establishes pattern of Federation assistance to allied intelligence

---

## 2022

**March 21, 2022** - [Shade of Gray](/esf/stories/shade-of-gray) (Investigation begins)
- Special Agent Marisa Comey (FBI Technology Crimes Division) finds Federation calling card on desk
- Jeane LeBouf (MTF-34) contacts FBI regarding domestic EMP threat
- Russian-origin EMP components traced through Eastern Seaboard ports
- Far-right extremist network identified targeting major metropolitan areas

**March 21-25, 2022** - [Shade of Gray](/esf/stories/shade-of-gray) (Investigation develops)
- ESF provides intelligence through CIPHER and Shade
- FBI identifies six priority locations across two states
- Signal encryption broken, offshore financial networks traced
- Twelve extremist cell members mapped through impossible-speed intelligence

**March 26, 2022** - [Shade of Gray](/esf/stories/shade-of-gray) (Newark interdiction)
- Final EMP component shipment arrives Newark docks
- MTF-34 deploys in powered armor alongside FBI tactical teams
- Agent James Hartfield witnesses Federation combat capabilities
- Seventeen suspects apprehended, multiple EMP devices secured

**March 26-27, 2022** - [Shade of Gray](/esf/stories/shade-of-gray) (San Francisco operation)
- Additional EMP devices located in San Francisco Mission District
- FBI team transported via Shade spacecraft to West Coast
- Orbital surveillance coordinates ground interdiction
- Q-cars carry FBI team across San Francisco at impossible speed

**March 27, 2022** - [Shade of Gray](/esf/stories/shade-of-gray) (Aftermath)
- Marisa Comey debriefed at FBI headquarters
- Case files prepared for federal prosecution by the U.S. Attorney's Office, Northern District of California
- AUSA Jennifer Park questions evidence timeline and methodology
- First documented case of Federation parallel construction concerns

**July 16, 2022** - [Shadows in Tel Aviv](/esf/stories/the-israeli-wraith)
- Israeli pressure on US for Federation technology access
- Diplomatic tensions over Wraith deployment
- Ambassador Levi demands explanations
- Israeli government request for Federation partnership denied
- Federation dossier delivered to Prime Minister David Abramov: "You need to do better"
- Abramov briefs the security cabinet in closed session; reform program begins
- Winter 2022–23: Wraith *Tsel* assigned to the Mossad

---

## 2023

**February 21, 2023** - [Silent Watchers](/esf/stories/the-japanese-wraith)
- Japanese Defense Intelligence Agency discusses North Korean provocations
- Recognition of Federation involvement with US and Israeli forces
- Growing awareness of Federation influence in regional security
- Colonel Yamada identifies technological disparities

---

## 2024

**March 19, 2024** - [P = NP](/esf/stories/p-np)
- Federation operative Mira Hale breaks 2048-bit RSA in seconds before researcher Ethan Walsh at a Cambridge, Massachusetts security conference
- Keynote speaker Dr. Richard Langford's live HTTPS session altered as a second demonstration
- Armed attack on the conference hotel; Mira steers events using quantum probability modeling
- All attackers neutralized with zero fatalities
- Walsh receives handwritten notes that seed research into quantum fault tolerance

**June 13, 2024** - [Angels in Armor](/esf/stories/angels-in-armor)
- Federation Landmates deploy to Gaza Strip
- First open peacekeeping intervention by Federation forces
- IDF encounters with Golem units
- UN emergency sessions regarding alien technology
- QR code treaty signed with UN - first formal acknowledgment

**June 17, 2024** - [Executive Authority](/esf/stories/executive-authority)
- Four civilian scientists drafted into the COLD FORGE study under classified Executive Order 13120/A
- President Biden briefs the team in person at Wright-Patterson AFB
- Team studies Gaza and Taiwan Strait evidence, including the Merkava hull and a *Haiyangshan* bridge section
- Key findings repeatedly vanish or fail in untraceable incidents; program stood down March 15, 2025

**December 6, 2024** - [Adrenaline](/esf/stories/adrenaline)
- Grand Concordance of Interstellar Medicine attacked
- Terror squad strikes Xenari medical facility
- Staff Sergeant Logan Carter and medical team respond
- Human physiological capabilities demonstrated to galactic community

---

## 2025

**January 7, 2025** - [Across the Sea](/esf/stories/across-the-sea)
- Chinese naval provocations in Taiwan Strait
- Captain Lu Jianjun leads rogue destroyer flotilla
- Federation intervention prevents missile attack on Taipei
- Landmates deployed on Taiwan beaches as deterrent

**March 1, 2025** - [David v Goliath](/esf/stories/david-v-goliath)
- HARBINGER's Landmate stops Colonel Pavlo Kovalenko's convoy in Transcarpathia and hands Ukraine a targeting coordinator
- Aurora strikes destroy the Ilovaisk rail junction (March 6) and three Russian staging areas in Donetsk Oblast (March 9), seen across Europe
- Warned by HARBINGER, Kyiv evacuates; Guardian batteries appear around five Ukrainian cities and stop every weapon in Russia's March 12 saturation strike on Kyiv
- HARBINGER gives Moscow a 72-hour withdrawal ultimatum; Medyka Accords signed March 14
- Russia begins a 90-day withdrawal from all occupied territory, including Crimea, with reparations
- Guardians recovered and the coordinator covertly retrieved at month's end

**March 14-November 15, 2025** - [Ex Mero Mutu](/esf/stories/ex-mero-mutu)
- Portugal withdraws from the F-35 program
- HARBINGER's Talons intercept a Portuguese F-16 patrol and land at Monte Real; offer of twenty-five Talons (May 21)
- Lieutenant Freitas flies a Talon demonstration to orbit and Jupiter (June 28)
- Portuguese cabinet accepts the offer; first five Talons arrive the next day (July 5)
- Portugal briefs NATO leadership in Brussels; Washington reacts (July 12)
- Defense Minister Carvalho tells the US ambassador Portugal expects to be treated as an equal (November 15)

**August 21, 2025** - [Torchlight](/esf/stories/torchlight)
- Pirate attack on joint Kryllian-Xenari freighter *Galara's Hope*
- Naomi Campbell and Leonard Park improvise defense
- First documented human engineering innovation in space
- Demonstrates human adaptability in galactic context

---

## 2026

**June 15, 2026** - [Flight of Icari](/esf/stories/flight-of-icari)
- Federation pilot Sergeant Nathaniel Voss's Talon defeats an F-22, Typhoon and Gripen in a mock engagement at Vandenberg Space Force Base
- NATO pilots Adams (USAF), Kraus (Luftwaffe) and Lindholm (Swedish Air Force) begin Talon conversion under liaison officer Mira Halden and the AI EMBER
- Trainees complete orbital, lunar, Martian and asteroid-belt combat trials, the first non-Federation pilots to qualify on warp travel
- Talons are observed by the PLAN submarine *Hǎishì Yǐng* off California and by Tiangong station
- An uncrewed Talon intercepts and destroys a Chinese surveillance balloon over the eastern Pacific
- Weeks later, USAF-marked Talons escort two PLAAF J-20s out of Taiwan's ADIZ; China circulates the footage and regional powers stand down

**September 3, 2026** - [The Helpers](/esf/stories/the-helpers) (UN Offer)
- HARBINGER interrupts UN General Assembly
- Offers humanitarian Landmates to member nations
- 162 nations accept in 90-second vote
- First large-scale technology transfer to civilian authorities

**September 4, 2026** - [The Helpers](/esf/stories/the-helpers) (Delivery)
- Landmates delivered worldwide at 0600 local time
- Unidentified black aircraft deploy units via jump jets
- No instructions or documentation provided
- Units stand motionless for three weeks while nations attempt analysis

**September 4, 2026** - [Executive Authority](/esf/stories/executive-authority) (Recall)
- COLD FORGE team recalled to study FEMA's Landmates at Camp Blanding
- Four cross-citing papers published; outside labs produce partial imitations

**September 29, 2026** - [The Helpers](/esf/stories/the-helpers) (Voice Control Discovery)
- FEMA logistics worker accidentally triggers voice command response
- Discovery spreads globally within 48 hours
- Natural language commands in any language confirmed functional
- Radio frequency monitoring capability discovered

**October 7, 2026** - [The Helpers](/esf/stories/the-helpers) (UN Coordination)
- UN establishes International Landmate Coordination Initiative
- Training programs and safety protocols developed
- Data-sharing network created among accepting nations
- 162 nations begin coordinated Landmate deployment

**November 18, 2026** - [The Helpers](/esf/stories/the-helpers) (Tokyo Earthquake)
- Magnitude 7.8 earthquake strikes Tokyo Bay
- Aftershock triggers Naoko Yoshika's discovery of piloting capability
- First human directly pilots Landmate in operational conditions
- 60+ lives saved during 4.5-hour continuous operation

**November 19, 2026** - [The Helpers](/esf/stories/the-helpers) (Viral Discovery)
- Mobile phone video of Yoshika entering cockpit goes viral
- Nearly 80 million views within a day
- Global realization that Landmates can be piloted
- UN calls emergency session on piloting protocols

**November 20, 2026** - [The Helpers](/esf/stories/the-helpers) (Training Program)
- Sergeant Naoko Yoshika promoted and assigned training development
- Becomes world's only certified Landmate pilot
- Tasked with creating international pilot training program
- 48-hour documentation period produces 37-page report

**November 22-24, 2026** - [The Helpers](/esf/stories/the-helpers) (First Training Class)
- 16 international cadets begin training at Chitose Air Base
- Two days ground instruction, two days practical training
- All 16 candidates successfully certified as pilots
- FEMA equipment operator Martinez becomes second certified pilot globally

**November 25, 2026** - [The Helpers](/esf/stories/the-helpers) (International Expansion)
- First trained pilots return to home nations
- EU announces training center in Germany
- Multiple nations establish pilot programs
- Japan plans 50 additional pilots by year end

**December 15, 2026** - [The Helpers](/esf/stories/the-helpers) (Weaponization Debate)
- US Army Transformation and Training Command debates arming Landmates
- Proposal for Bofors 40mm autocannon configuration
- Major General Granger argues against militarization
- Decision made to maintain peaceful-use restriction for six months

**December 18, 2026** - [Executive Authority](/esf/stories/executive-authority) (Fort Hood)
- Federation team infiltrates Fort Hood and warns Major General David Chen against arming Landmates
- Chen withdraws the autocannon proposal

## 2027

**March 7, 2027** - [Knock Knock](/esf/stories/knock-knock) (Ryzak Intelligence Operation)
- Ryzak operative Kresh'tar infiltrates Xenari frontier world bar
- Commander Vael'thi's comm unit compromised via holographic disguise
- Encryption keys for Xenari fleet coordination channels stolen
- Intelligence theft enables subsequent Ryzak invasion planning

**March 9, 2027** - [Knock Knock](/esf/stories/knock-knock) (Battle of Sol)
- Ryzak battle fleet enters Sol system via Jupiter cynosural beacon
- Thirty-four warships, including ten capital ships, attack Federation positions
- Commander Sarah Chen activates experimental FLYCATCHER system at Galileo Station
- Gravity disruption field prevents all warp travel in 250km radius
- Thirteen Earth pilots killed, including Americans Patterson, Lee, and Vasquez
- Ryzak fleet destroyed by Third North Fleet reinforcements
- First demonstration of warp disruption capability in galactic warfare

**March 12, 2027** - [Knock Knock](/esf/stories/knock-knock) (Arlington Ceremony)
- Memorial service held at Arlington National Cemetery
- President Trump presides over memorial for fallen Earth pilots
- Three empty graves for pilots whose bodies remain at Jupiter
- Families presented with commemorative coins and metal Talon statuettes
- Inscribed with "Semper Inter Astra" (Always Among the Stars)

**July 18, 2027** - [Knock Knock](/esf/stories/knock-knock) (Jovian Shipyards Commissioning)
- FSS Flycatcher (destroyer-class) commissioned at Ganymede orbit
- FSS Broadsword (cruiser-class) commissioned alongside
- Both vessels equipped with warp disruption technology
- SUNTZU confirms no galactic power possesses counter-measures
- Ryzak leadership unable to investigate loss due to CONCORD restrictions
- Classified commissioning attended only by Battle of Sol veterans
- Broadsword deployed to permanent station at Galileo
- Flycatcher assigned to outer Jupiter system patrol

## Training Programs

**June 2026-Present** - [Flight of Icari](/esf/stories/flight-of-icari)
- Federation pilot training program at Vandenberg
- Introduction of Talon-class fighters with FTL capability
- Mars and asteroid belt combat scenarios
- Advanced aerospace warfare development

**November 2026-Present** - [The Helpers](/esf/stories/the-helpers)
- International Landmate pilot training program
- Multiple national training centers established
- Standardized certification through UN Initiative
- Hundreds of pilots anticipated by early 2027

## Key Milestones

- **1947**: Foundation event - Roswell Incident
- **2021**: First covert operations with allies
- **2022**: Major domestic counterterrorism operation (EMP threat)
- **2022**: First FBI formal cooperation with Federation
- **2024**: Open intervention begins (Gaza deployment)
- **2024**: Formal UN recognition achieved
- **2025**: Direct military intervention (Taiwan Strait)
- **2025**: Russo-Ukrainian War ends under HARBINGER's pressure (Medyka Accords)
- **2025**: Deep space operations with galactic allies
- **2026**: Large-scale civilian technology transfer (Landmates)
- **2026**: First human-piloted Landmate (Tokyo)
- **2026**: International pilot training program established
- **2027**: Battle of Sol - first major alien invasion of Sol system
- **2027**: Warp disruption technology demonstrated and weaponized
- **2027**: First Federation warships designed around human-discovered physics
- **2027**: Humanity admitted to CONCORD as a member species
