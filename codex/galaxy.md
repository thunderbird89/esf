## Aliens
Much of the galaxy is in the sphere of influence of one of the four great races:
- The (mostly) benevolent _Xenari_ who delight in guiding younger races
- The belligerent _Ryzak_ who thrive on conquest to feed their empire
- The enigmatic _Seraphyn_ who mostly prefer their philosophical debates to active engagement
- The industrious _Kryllians_ whose mass-production facilities and merchant vessels keep the galaxy's economy running

Earth and the Sol system happens to lie in a "border zone" of sorts between the Xenari and Ryzak spheres of influence, and is of interest to both parties for reasons not currently known to the Federation.

Interestingly, humans are the only race that possesses an evolved flight-or-fight response. No other race, not even the Ryzak, have a biological system equivalent to the human adrenal response, although most militaries use injectable stimulants that can temporarily mimic such an effect, although these systems rely on external injectors.

### [The Xenari]({{ '/foundations/galaxy/xenari' | relative_url }})
A tall, frail, bioluminescent reptilian species who lack an adrenal response. Organized into a consensus-driven, guild-based society, they act as the Federation's benefactors under a "mentorship clause" of their non-interventionist Stewardship Compact.

### [The Seraphyn]({{ '/foundations/galaxy/seraphyn' | relative_url }})
A tall, telepathic species whose civilization favors philosophy and subtle influence over governance or conquest, wielding technology that rivals or surpasses the Xenari's.

### [The Kryllians]({{ '/foundations/galaxy/kryllians' | relative_url }})
A short, stocky, ammonia-breathing species from the high-gravity world of Draxyl-4, serving as the galaxy's industrial backbone through unmatched manufacturing and merchant fleets.

### [The Ryzak]({{ '/foundations/galaxy/ryzak' | relative_url }})
A belligerent, expansionist reptilian empire built on trial-by-combat and clan hierarchy, viewing Earth as a contested prize within reach of their conquest-driven ambitions.

### [Alien technology]({{ '/foundations/galaxy/alien-technology' | relative_url }})
An overview of the stargate network, jump drives, and cynosural beacons that the Federation relies on for interstellar reach, despite its comparatively shallow grasp of alien technology as a whole.

## [Galactic politics]({{ '/foundations/galaxy/galactic-politics' | relative_url }})
CONCORD, the great races' governing body, has declared Earth a protected conflict zone, sharply limiting how the Xenari, Ryzak, and other star empires may act within the Sol system.
