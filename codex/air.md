## [VSX-7 Talon]({{ '/foundations/air/talon' | relative_url }})
The Federation's latest multi-role, multi-mode aircraft, able to operate in air, space, and underwater within the same mission, with a variable-geometry airframe and a sub-orbital boost escape maneuver.

## [VPA-5 Aeon]({{ '/foundations/air/aeon' | relative_url }})
The *Extreme Deep Invader*, a five-plane lifting-body stealth strike aircraft armed with a nose-mounted MASER emitter, built for precision deep-penetration missions in hostile territory.

## [VGA-3 Scorpion]({{ '/foundations/air/scorpion' | relative_url }})
An aerial gunship hybridizing the roles of the Apache and Mil Mi-24 Hind, combining chin-turreted plasma cannon fire support with troop and Landmate transport capacity.

## [DAA-2 Aurora]({{ '/foundations/air/aurora' | relative_url }})
A non-stealthy, post-hypersonic attack drone that outruns anti-air defenses through sheer speed, striking with its Stiletto fast missiles before egressing at speeds that wrap it in a plasma sheath.

## [VGA-6 Dragon Heavy Attack Gunship]({{ '/foundations/air/dragon' | relative_url }})
A heavy-frame gunship built for brute-force aerial dominance, bristling with missile banks, plasma cannons, and turrets, and capable of deploying both infantry and Landmates into contested landing zones.
