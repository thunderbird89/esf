## Nanotechnology in the galaxy

The benefactors have not granted the Federation wide access to nanotechnology. In fact, self-replicating nanotechnology is considered a highly dangerous technology throughout the galaxy, and is one of the few things all four Great Races agree on heavily restricting without much debate.

## Non-replicating nanotechnology

In light of the heavy restrictions, the Xenari have only allowed the Federation access to non-self-replicating nanotech, mostly in the form of *utility fog*.

*Utility Fog* is a form of nanotechnology composed of independent nanites that link up to form larger-scale structures from a dispersed aerosol. This allows the user to instantly create tools and implements out of mostly thin air.  
These tools are reconfigurable instantly, using the controller associated with the nanite canister, allowing users to change the shape immediately as needed.

### Fixed-form nanotechnology

The most widespread use of nanotechnology in the Federation is *fixed-form nanotech*, where the nanites are held in suspension, ready to be applied, and on activation solidify into a pre-determined structure.

This allows the creation of quick-deploy implements like wound dressings that fuse to the damaged area and immediately stabilize even catastrophic wounds (including complete amputations).

### Nanotech tools

Nanites are also leveraged for their ability to alter matter, not just to act as "programmable matter".

Nanite-based tools and machinery are often used to shape and process raw materials and feedstock into final shapes, especially for construction, where raw feedstock is rapidly processed into structural shells, utilizing precise geometric lattices to achieve strength-to-weight ratios impossible with regular concrete and steel.
