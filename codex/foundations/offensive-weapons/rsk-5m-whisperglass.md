Federation magnetic accelerator rifles already outrange almost every conventional firearm on Earth by virtue of muzzle velocity alone, but for engagements where even that isn't distant enough, the Federation fields a dedicated designated sniper rifle: the RSK-5/M *Whisperglass*.

## Ammunition
The *Whisperglass* is a railgun in the strict sense — a magnetic accelerator rather than a chemically-propelled weapon — and draws on the same thermal clip technology fielded across the rest of the Federation arsenal to manage its waste heat. Five rounds can be fired in quick succession before the clip saturates and needs to be swapped or allowed to vent.

## Weapon system
The rifle is a compact design comparable in size to most designated marksman rifles, but built around magnetic repulsors running the length of the upper receiver and barrel. Squeezing the trigger to its first stage locks the target under the reticle and levitates the barrel, physically decoupling it from the receiver so that small hand movements can no longer throw off aim. From that point, the rifle automatically tracks the target within a 5º cone for as long as the trigger stays at first stage; only a further squeeze actually fires the round. The result is a rifle that behaves, in the moment of the shot, more like a stabilized turret than a handheld weapon.

Its stated range exceeds 5,000 meters, with a lethal range extending as far as 15,000 meters at the cost of some accuracy at the extreme end.

## Optics and targeting
The builtin scope switches between visible-light, night vision, and thermal imaging, with up to 5x optical zoom. A laser rangefinder establishes distance to target automatically, and the rifle's ballistic computer folds that range in with crosswind, Coriolis force, potential ricochets, and target movement, overlaying a corrected aimpoint for the shooter once it acquires a target.

## Deployment
The *Whisperglass* is the Federation's answer to engagements that call for a confirmed kill at a distance no conventional rifle, or even a standard-issue magnetic rifle, can reliably reach. Its stabilized-barrel handling and long reach have made it a particular favorite of covert operations teams, where a single, unhurried shot from far outside an area's expected engagement envelope is often worth more than any amount of sustained fire.
