The Federation employs shield generators that create constrained energy fields capable of being shaped according to the subject needs. They diffract/disrupt energy weapons like laser and plasma bolts, and deflect physical projectiles, even various hypervelocity rounds employed by extraterrestrial forces.

## Shield strength

Shields are generally rated by the amount of energy they absorb or deflect. Personal shields range from 25 KJ on the lower end (easily capable of blocking a 50 BMG round at point blank range), all the way to 75 KJ; vehicle-mounted shields may go well into the megajoules, while space-capable shields can easily reach the petajoule range (capable of tanking nuclear explosions) even before various shield support modules are installed.

## Operation

Shields draw very little power at standby, making it easy to sustain them indefinitely when not under fire. However, when the field is being regenerated after taking damage, the power draw can get quite severe. High burst damage may even burn out the generator, necessitating quick field repairs or switching to an alternative generator to maintain the field.

## Drawbacks

Shields are not without drawbacks, though. They don't make their subject completely invulnerable, as very low-energy phenomena can bypass them.

Such low-energy phenomena include things like hand-thrown rocks or melee attacks, which pass through the shields without being deflected and may still damage the object underneath.
