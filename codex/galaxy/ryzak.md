The _Ryzak_ are a belligerent, expansionist reptilian empire, one of the galaxy's four great races, built on warfare, subjugation, and the pursuit of honor through combat — a philosophy that shapes everything from their politics to their day-to-day lives.

## Physiology
Ryzak evolved on a harsh, high-gravity world of extreme temperatures and relentless predators, and it shows: thick scaly hides that can turn small-arms fire, powerful limbs tipped with razor claws, and reinforced bone structure built for close combat. Their fangs still carry a vestige of that evolutionary past in the form of a paralytic venom, secondary eyelids shield their eyes from bright flashes and environmental hazards, and redundant organ structures make them unusually hard to kill outright.

They are strict carnivores, and their culture glorifies the hunt, including the ritualistic consumption of defeated enemies. A Ryzak can live up to 250 years, though few survive that long given how their culture treats combat.

## Society and Culture
Ryzak society runs on a single principle: strength determines worth. The weak are subjugated or eliminated, and every position of power is won through combat trial rather than inherited or appointed.

### Clans
Ryzak society is organized into competing Clans, kinship groups that double as its basic military and political unit; a Ryzak's Clan affiliation matters more to their standing than any individual rank they hold within it. Many Clans have developed loose specializations over generations of internal culling and cross-breeding — some are renowned for naval crews, others for shock infantry, still others for the genetic engineering programs that produce the empire's specialized warrior castes — though every Clan maintains general-purpose forces regardless of its focus, since a Clan that can only fight one kind of war is a Clan waiting to be conquered by one that can fight several.

### The Supreme Warlord
At the head of the empire sits the Supreme Warlord, a title held by whichever Clan currently proves itself strongest through the _Ascension_, a rolling contest of martial prowess and strategic maneuvering between Clan leaders rather than a single duel. An Ascension can take years to resolve and often involves proxy conflicts between allied Clans as much as direct combat, and a reigning Warlord who shows any sign of weakening can expect a new Ascension to open against them well before their natural death. The role carries no fixed term and no ceremonial function — a Warlord who stops winning stops being Warlord.

### The honor code
Disputes of any kind — political, personal, judicial — are typically settled through ritualized duels rather than debate, and losing one costs a Ryzak standing rather than necessarily their life; outright execution is reserved for cowardice, not defeat. Ritual consumption of a defeated enemy, where practical, is considered the highest mark of respect a Ryzak can pay an opponent — an acknowledgment that the loser's strength is being taken into the victor rather than simply discarded — and refusing the practice against a worthy foe is itself read as a small dishonor to both parties.

### Diplomacy through strength
Ryzak do not negotiate from a position of weakness, and treat diplomacy that isn't backed by a credible show of force as beneath contempt. They will, however, respect an adversary who matches their strength, and defeated populations or individuals who prove themselves capable in Ryzak terms are sometimes offered a place within the empire rather than subjugation outright — a rare avenue by which outsiders can earn genuine Ryzak standing, though it demands they keep proving it for as long as they hold it.

## Technology
Ryzak ships and vehicles favor mass and firepower over agility or stealth, built as armored juggernauts rather than precision instruments. Elite warriors are selected through live combat trials, and where natural strength falls short, the Ryzak are willing to engineer it — selective breeding and genetic modification produce specialized warrior castes for the empire's armies.

Their preferred mode of space combat is direct boarding action: heavily armored shock troops breach an enemy vessel and settle the engagement in brutal melee, rather than relying on standoff weapons to win a fight at range.

## Relations with Other Races
- _Xenari_: open ideological opposition. The Ryzak respect Xenari technology, but read their pacifism as weakness rather than restraint.
- _Seraphyn_: baffling and "honorless" by Ryzak standards, though even the Ryzak concede their intelligence.
- _Kryllians_: tolerated as a necessary evil — the Ryzak have little patience for non-warrior species, but rely on Kryllian suppliers regardless.
- Humans: an upstart species with untapped potential, sitting in a contested border region between Ryzak and Xenari influence — worth conquering, or on rare occasions, worth recruiting. CONCORD's protected-zone ruling keeps the Ryzak from acting on either impulse for now.
