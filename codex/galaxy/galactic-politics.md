The galaxy's peace and security is maintained by the _Consolidated Cooperations and Relations Command_, aka CONCORD, composed of delegates from the four great races. CONCORD has sweeping powers to act in the interest of stability, including military sanctions on any of the great races (and by extension, the younger races too). As such, no parties question its authority.

Younger species can also be admitted to CONCORD as member species. Humanity, represented by the EarthSpace Federation, became the newest of them in the months after the Battle of Sol in 2027. That October, a Federation officer lectured at the CONCORD War College for the first time.
