# Source review: decisions log

What was done with `source-review.md`, for checking before translation. Like the review, this file is a working note, not for publication. Every ticked item was applied unless it is listed under "Not applied" below. This log records the author decisions, the places where a fix departed from its proposal, and everything that added to canon.

## New canon to confirm

These are the decisions that add to the world rather than just correcting it.

- Humanity became a CONCORD member species in the months after the Battle of Sol, and a Federation officer first lectured at the CONCORD War College in October 2027 (TLN-03; `galactic-politics.md`, timeline milestone).
- Federation interstellar travel (CX-02, KK-27). Federation ships carry warp drives only, with no jump drives, even on capital ships. They use the public stargates between systems, and enter or leave Sol only through jump bridges opened to the Xenari beacon near Jupiter (`ftl.md`, `space.md`).
- *The Beginning* (1947):
  - Thel'zar is now a canonical Xenari: over two meters tall, frail, pale translucent blue skin with glowing markings, and no breathing mask. It speaks throughout in a borrowed Southern human voice.
  - The "Great Circle of my people" replaces the "Terran Advisory Council".
  - The commander acts "pending the Great Circle's review".
  - The Pentagon briefing has no stenographer and no minutes.
  - Theodore von Kármán replaces Goddard, who died in 1945, and Georges Bidault is now the French founder.
- *Shadows in Tel Aviv*:
  - The Federation dossier holds observation logs from the May 2021 war only, with no Landmates or interventions before 2024.
  - Abramov responds in a closed security-cabinet session rather than the Knesset.
  - *Tsel* arrives in winter 2022–23.
- Codex additions requested by the stories:
  - Ryzak mandible-like jaw plates.
  - Kryllian chitin plates, compound eyes, nasal vents, antennae and mandibles.
  - Zai'nor fleet crews whose roles outsiders render as naval ranks.
  - The FSS hull prefix for Federation warships.
  - The new variant letters M, K, L, B and P.
  - Non-lethal incapacitator rounds and less-lethal rifle rounds.
  - The Wraith's six payload drones and sub-orbital mode.
  - Named shackled AIs (Hermes, Tsel, Nue).
  - EMBER.
  - The Talon's warp drive, armament and allied loans.
  - The Landmate's visor-band head and folding rail cannon.
  - The Aurora targeting coordinator.
- *Knock Knock*:
  - A fictional Joint Chiefs roster (Ashford, Ostrowski, Kincaid, Albright, Brandt, Holloway).
  - Kraveth survives aboard *Worldbreaker*, which holds the bridge open.
  - The theft moves to 7 March, and the front matter is now 2027-03-07.
- Many minor characters were renamed to remove name clashes across stories. The story sections below list them.
